  • Close the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor (only one program can use the port).

  • Nothing found: SDA and SCL swapped, or the parts have no 3.3V / GND.

Checks
  • Always "–" (no echo): HC-SR04 VCC must be 5V; check TRIG on 20 and the ECHO path ECHO → 1 kΩ → 21, plus 21 → 2 kΩ → G.
  • Stuck on one number: something is right in front of it, or the sensor faces a wall.
  • Jumpy: soft or angled surfaces scatter sound. That's normal.

Button not registering?
  • Press the stick straight down until it clicks. Tilting doesn't count.
  • SW (sometimes KEY or SEL) goes to pin 3: the 5th hole down on the right header (5V, G, 3.3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0).
  • Multimeter in beep mode on the module's SW and GND pins: it should beep while pressed. No beep = bad button; use a spare joystick.
  • If a pin finder chip lights up, move the wire from that pin to pin 3.
  • "button" stuck on pressed: the SW wire is touching GND somewhere.

Test and tune it
  • Hold the cane tip 5–10 cm above a table: should say FLOOR SEEN.
  • Slide it past the table edge: should say NO FLOOR and count a hole alarm. Then bring it back.
  • Mount the IR about 5 cm above the cane tip, pointing straight down. A stair step or curb then puts the floor 20–25 cm away, beyond a 15 cm trigger.
  • The screw on the module sets the trigger height. Turn it a quarter turn and lift again. The page shows whether you went the right way.
  • FC-51 modules reach about 15–25 cm on light floors and less on dark ones. TCRT5000 modules only reach 1–3 cm, so they can't do this.
  • Black carpet and bright sunlight weaken IR. Test on the floor you'll demo on.
  • False alarms while walking? Each cane lift shows up as "last no-floor". Set HOLE_CONFIRM_MS in smart_cane.ino just above your typical lift (120 → 250 ms).
  • Flips when you wave a hand from far away but not over edges? That's a PIR motion sensor, not an IR obstacle sensor.
